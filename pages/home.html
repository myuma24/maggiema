About Me

Since I can remember, art has always been my first language. As a child, I struggled to express my emotions verbally, so I turned to my sketchbook to speak for me.

However, as a first-generation student, I knew that my outlet as an artist wasn't a practical path (and one my parents weren't so fond of). My parents encouraged me to think beyond just a canvas, and I learned to channel my lifelong creative intuition into UI/UX Design.

My goal is to create spaces where users feel understood, much like how art once helped me understand myself. In my work, I strive to empathize first, identifying pain points to elevate the user experience and minimize the hurdles within a design.

Education: Earned my Bachelor of Arts in Design and Bachelor of Arts in Digital Media from UC Davis.
