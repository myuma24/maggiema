My Education

Bachelor of Arts from UC Davis

I graduated from UC Davis in 2024 with a Bachelor of Arts in Design and Cinema & Digital Media. I had the opportunity to explore a wide range of design disciplines, from graphic design to interaction design and everything in between.

I honed a deep empathy for the user experience and built the technical foundation that launched my career as a UI/UX designer.

My Experience

Senior Design Associate

Currently, I work as a Senior Design Associate at DigitalNest.

This role has cemented how much I love my work; the process from design to development is where the work starts to breathe. It is incredibly rewarding to build something from the groupd up, and know that what we’ve created is going to go out in the world and help people.
