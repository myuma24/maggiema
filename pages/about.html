My Education

Bachelor of Arts from UC Davis

I graduated from UC Davis in 2024 with a Bachelor of Arts in Design and Cinema & Digital Media. currently exploring Master programs in HCI

I had the opportunity to explore a wide range of design disciplines, from graphic design to interaction design and developed a deep empathy for the user experience that launched my career as a UI/UX designer.

My Experience

2+ Years in UI/UX 6 if you count education!

Currently, I work as a Senior Design Associate at DigitalNest.

This role has cemented how much I love my work; the process from design to development is incredibly rewarding and the team I had the honor to work with made it clear to me that this is the career I want to pursue.
