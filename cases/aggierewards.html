UI/UX CASE STUDY

DI Challenge: AggieRewards

OVERVIEW

A reimagined platform that bridges the gap between education and career for designers. It fosters professional growth through weekly challenges, niche communities, and a unique creative networking swipe feature. By blending a portfolio showcase with a welcoming community, Hubbble empowers users to sharpen their skills and build lasting industry connections.

MY ROLE

I collaborated with two designers to research and build Hubbble during an 8-hour designathon at UC Davis. I served as one of the main designers, conducting user interviews, brainstorming initial designs to final prototype.

DEFINE

AggieRewards was the product of a three week long design sprint hosted by Design Interactive at UC Davis. Our choices for this design sprint was to redesign Instagram Threads or develop a rewards app for UC Davis’ aggie rewards. We chose to design a rewards app.

How might we… “design a mobile app that enhances the dining experience for UC Davis students by incentivizing and rewarding visiting on-campus food destinations?”

RESEARCH

Loyalty Program

Before user research, we sought to understand what made loyalty programs so effective. We researched articles on different types of loyalty programs and found the four essential E’s; Entice, Engage, Enhance, and Encourage. Below are some additional key points we took note on when designing our app.

Interviews

We also conducted user interviews with four participants about their experience with career and education development and how they currently navigate network growth and education development.

Interview Summary

From our interviews we found that:

1. NETWORKING

Building connections play an important role in both personal and career growth, especially when the connection feels genuine.

2. SKILL DEVELOPMENT

People are motivated to grow their skills through projects especially when opportunities align with their interests.

3. FEEDBACK

Users value spaces where knowledge and experience can be shared naturally through feedback & mentorship.

Competitive Audit

I performed competitive analysis on platforms offering users opportunities and events for personal growth and networking. I identified standout features and effective strategies between each platform and discussed how we can incorporate similar features into our app.

Develop

How Might We

We began with a series of “How might we” questions to explore different directions and narrow in on what would be most meaningful for users.

Design Direction

We focused on designing a platform that makes connection and growth feel natural. Hubbble brings together weekly design challenges, community spaces called Hubbbles, and a swipe-based matching feature to support feedback, collaboration, and meaningful connections.

Wireframes

With our tight deadline, I began by identifying what key aspects of the app we wanted to showcase, allowing us to divide and conquer the task. I created a clear checklist of screens to be developed:

  • Home Page: The main hub for a weekly design challenge and hot "Hubbbles" (communities) the algorithm believes the user may be interested in.
  • Connection Page: A fun swipe to connect feature for expanding network; a easy, friendlier, more casual way for users to connect and strip away the layer of awkwardness
  • Profile Page: This page acts as a built in portfolio where users can showcase their work.
  • The "Hubbbles": Where all the communities and groups are located. Built for users to find the specific groups they're looking for, such as project groups or people who want portfolio or resume review. This social media setting would allow users to authentically connect and develop in their career.

Design System

We focused on creating a fun, casual app for users to feel comfortable conversing and supporting each other naturally. Implementing a space theme based on the Hubble Telescope because of Hubble’s many achievements and contributions to space discovery. We thought this idea encompassed our goals of creating a space to learn, achieve, and contribute to many great things.

Next Steps

I would continue expanding and refining Hubbble’s core functionality to support deeper engagement and long-term use.

Next steps would include:

  • Expanding additional app features that were out of scope during the designathon
  • Designing full task flows and high-fidelity prototypes for:
    • Posting and joining forums
    • Submitting and participating in weekly design challenges
    • Creating and managing profile content
  • Further exploring edge cases and user journeys to strengthen scalability and usability

Additionally, I would like to expand on the mentor and mentee experience within the app. The user personas provided during the designathon expressed interest in mentorship and peer-to-peer learning opportunities, but time constraints prevented us from fully exploring this feature.

This feature would include

  • Encouraging users to apply as mentors for weekly challenges
  • Selecting a rotating group of mentors to guide participants through each challenge
  • Allowing mentees to learn directly from peers in a structured, low-pressure environment
